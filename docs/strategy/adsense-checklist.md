# AdSense Approval Checklist — LawsuitsClaim.com

## Content Requirements
- [ ] At least 35–50 high-quality original articles published (target: 45)
- [ ] No duplicate content, copied legal text, or paraphrased settlement pages
- [ ] No thin articles (each must be 800+ words minimum, targets are 1,200–2,200)
- [ ] No fake author credentials, invented bar memberships, or fabricated awards
- [ ] Legal disclaimer visible on every article page
- [ ] All articles include sources to verify section

## Required Pages Published
- [ ] Homepage (complete with disclaimer)
- [ ] About page (clearly states not a law firm)
- [ ] Contact page (with legal advice warning)
- [ ] Editorial Policy
- [ ] Legal Disclaimer page
- [ ] Privacy Policy (mentions cookies and AdSense)
- [ ] Terms of Use
- [ ] Cookie Policy
- [ ] Sitemap page (or XML sitemap link visible)

## Navigation and Structure
- [ ] Clear top navigation with all main categories
- [ ] Footer links to all legal pages
- [ ] Breadcrumb navigation on article pages
- [ ] No broken links (run link checker before submission)
- [ ] No empty categories (each category has at least 5 articles)
- [ ] Category pages display article listings

## Technical SEO
- [ ] HTTPS enabled (SSL certificate active)
- [ ] Fast loading pages (target < 3 seconds on mobile)
- [ ] Mobile-friendly layout (verify in Google Mobile-Friendly Test)
- [ ] Search Console connected and verified
- [ ] XML sitemap submitted to Search Console
- [ ] robots.txt configured (no disallow on content pages)
- [ ] No 404 errors on key pages
- [ ] All canonical URLs set correctly
- [ ] OG images set for homepage and articles

## Content Quality
- [ ] No aggressive affiliate links (Phase 1 = AdSense only)
- [ ] No misleading claim forms or fake eligibility checkers
- [ ] No excessive ads (0 ads before approval)
- [ ] No popups before approval
- [ ] No fake trust badges or invented credentials
- [ ] No stock photos of lawyers in aggressive legal settings
- [ ] No sensational headlines or fake urgency language

## Legal and Compliance
- [ ] Clear disclaimer that site is not a law firm on every page
- [ ] Privacy policy covers Google Analytics and AdSense
- [ ] Cookie policy published
- [ ] No collection of sensitive legal case details

## Final Pre-Submission Steps
- [ ] Run Lighthouse audit — score 80+ on Performance and Accessibility
- [ ] Test on iPhone and Android mobile devices
- [ ] Test all internal links manually
- [ ] Verify all article pages render correctly
- [ ] Confirm sitemap at lawsuitsclaim.com/sitemap.xml returns valid XML
- [ ] Confirm robots.txt at lawsuitsclaim.com/robots.txt is correct
- [ ] Verify Search Console shows no crawl errors
- [ ] Deploy to production and let site run for 2–4 weeks before applying

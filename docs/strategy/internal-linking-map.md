# Internal Linking Map — LawsuitsClaim.com

## Hub and Spoke Architecture

Category pages are hubs. Articles are spokes. Every article links to its hub (category page) via breadcrumbs. Articles within the same category link to 2–3 related articles. Cross-category links are made where genuinely relevant.

## Meta Description Formula

**Pattern:** [What the reader will learn] + [primary keyword] + [trust signal or action prompt]. Keep under 160 characters.

**Examples:**

"A class action lawsuit lets a group of people with similar claims sue together. Learn how class actions work, who can join, and what to expect." (143 chars)

"Received a settlement notice in the mail? Learn what it means, whether it is legitimate, and what your options are before responding." (134 chars)

"Insurance claim denials are not always final. Learn the most common denial reasons and how to appeal each one." (110 chars)

## Title Length Rules
- Target: 50–60 characters
- Maximum: 65 characters (Google truncates above this)
- Always include primary keyword
- Avoid clickbait, question marks for evergreen content unless natural

## Schema Type Guidelines
- Standard educational articles: "Article"
- Articles with a prominent FAQ section: "FAQPage" (use FAQPage schema only when FAQs are visible on page)
- Homepage: WebSite + Organization
- Category pages: no article schema, BreadcrumbList only

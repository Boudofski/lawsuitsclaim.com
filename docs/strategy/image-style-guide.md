# Image Style Guide — LawsuitsClaim.com

## Brand Art Direction

**Aesthetic:** Clean, editorial, serious. Inspired by legal information publishers and reputable news sites — not settlement farm landing pages.

**Color palette in images:**
- Primary: Navy blue (#0f2044), white, light gray
- Accent: Brand blue (#1d4ed8), muted slate
- Avoid: Red urgency colors, green money imagery, sensational orange

**What to avoid in all images:**
- Stock photos of lawyers shaking hands
- Gavels as the primary visual (overused and cliché)
- Scales of justice as the only legal symbol
- Courtroom drama imagery
- Stock photos of people looking stressed or victimized
- Dollar bills or money bags
- Anything that looks like a settlement farm advertisement

## OG Image Style

All OG images use the programmatic template already implemented in the codebase:
- Dark navy background (#0f2044)
- Category label in light blue uppercase
- Article title in large white bold text
- "Plain-English legal information · Not legal advice" footer in slate gray
- No photography, no stock images

## Accessibility Requirements

All images must have descriptive alt text. Decorative illustrations should have `aria-hidden="true"`. Never use text in images as the primary content source — it cannot be read by screen readers or search engines.
